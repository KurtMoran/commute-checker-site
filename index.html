FAQ

Questions

Can't I just check a maps app?

You can check one departure time at a time. A full picture of one route is about 240 lookups: every 15 minutes, morning and evening, seven days. The report does all of them and shows you the pattern, including which days are worse and exactly when traffic builds and clears.

Where do the numbers come from?

Predicted drive times from a historical traffic model, by day of week and time of day, for your exact route. It's a typical week. One-off crashes, weather and events aren't in it.

How accurate is it?

It reflects typical conditions. Expect real days to vary by a few minutes either way, and more during holidays or major events. The shape of the curve (when it builds, when it peaks, which days are worst) is the reliable part.

How long can I view my report?

Each time your report opens, it runs about 240 live traffic lookups. To keep costs and computing down, it opens 3 times in 30 days. Take a screenshot to keep your copy.

How fast do I get it?

Within 24 hours of ordering, usually much sooner.

What about transit, biking or school drop-off?
